01 · Logo

The mark and the name

The mark is the first Calibra gauge, simplified, in the line style of the INTEGRATES tool icons: Performa's dial as a violet C open to the right, a ring hub and a diagonal needle, and an orange flame in the opening. The flame stands for heating, the gas bills the model is calibrated onto. The name is set in Sora 600, violet, with the dot on the i in orange: Sora's own round dot, in its own place.

Clear space and size

  • Keep free space of a quarter of the mark's width on every side.
  • Smallest mark: 16 px on screen, 5 mm in print.
  • Smallest logo: 96 px or 25 mm wide.

Do not

  • recolour the flame or the dot on the i
  • stretch, rotate or outline the logo
  • place it on the map or on busy pictures
  • set the name in another typeface

02 · Colour

Two colours, one meaning each

Calibra uses the INTEGRATES colours, violet and orange, on white and light grey. Each colour always means the same thing, in the logo, the interface and the charts.

Writing

  • Plain words from the user's side: "Room temperature", not parameter names.
  • Sentence case; numbers with their unit: 19.4 °C, 5.0 h, 27,945 kWh.
  • Buildings stay anonymous: 01 Office, 02 School, 03 Office complex.

Charts

  • The measured bills: soft grey bars with a darker top edge.
  • Your settings: a dashed orange line.
  • Optimised: a solid violet line.

Interface

  • White panels with light grey cards; thin lines, no heavy shadows.
  • One black main button per panel ("Use optimised", with the mark).
  • Icons from Lucide, 16 px, line style.

Waiting for the model

  • A small, real Bayesian optimisation plays: run dots, the fitted curve, its uncertainty band narrowing.
  • The logo's needle swings and settles while something loads.

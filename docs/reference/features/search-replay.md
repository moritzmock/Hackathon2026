# Search replay

[Knowledge base](../../index.md) · [Features](index.md)

**Type:** Reference · Answers: what does the landing page's “How the search found the settings” show, where do its numbers come from, and how are they checked?

The landing page replays our best calibration run, `all_12__ard_bayesian_optimisation__budget48__2018+2019__b01+b02+b03__w12__afef0.5` (overall 97.55), stage by stage, with real data only. A switch picks the building. The replay starts by itself once scrolled into view, once, and shows the finished search, still, when the device asks for reduced motion; play, pause and a position slider sit under it.

- **Score of each try** (left): the 48 tries against their number, the score on the y-axis, the best score so far as a rising step line and the best try as a ringed dark-violet dot. The three stages are shaded behind and named.
- **Real bills against the simulation** (right): the four scored months (February and March, 2018 and 2019), the real bills in ink and the simulation in violet. It shows the default settings during stage 1, then the best try after each finished stage; the violet bars grow or shrink to the next one, so they close in on the bills.
- **Score at each stage** (below): default settings, best after 1 · Spread out, best after 2 · Focus, best after 3 · Fine-tune (building 01: 38.76, 80.16, 93.75, 96.44).
- **The stage's explanation**, with the 6 settings held still in Focus and, when known, how many of the method's picks repeated an earlier whole-hour heating schedule.

**The method's stages** (`ard_bayesian_optimisation`): 1. spread out: try 1 is the model's default settings, tries 2–13 a Latin hypercube over the 12 settings; 2. focus: the 6 settings with the shortest fitted length scales are searched, the other 6 stay at the best try's values (one batch); 3. fine-tune: all 12 settings, batch by batch, by expected improvement. The method proposes 12 tries per batch; the organizer's objective does not run or log a try whose heating schedule rounds to the same whole hours as an earlier one, so a logged batch can be shorter (building 01's Focus: 12 picked, 6 run).

**Where the numbers come from:**

- `data/evaluation_trace.csv`: the run's evaluation trace (byte-identical to the backend's, given by the owner).
- `scripts/bake_search_replay.py` (`npm run replay`) writes `src/searchReplay/searchReplay.json`: each try's score and whether it is the final candidate, and the three stages, read from the trace (13 spread-out tries by the method's rule; Focus = the following tries that keep the same 6 settings at the best spread-out try's values). It also replays the method with the backend's own code (imported read-only from the run's `calibration_snapshot.py`, scikit-learn 1.9.1) and the run's seed (2026 + building number − 1) against the trace, to count the Focus batch's picks and repeats. Buildings 01 and 03 replay exactly; building 02 does not from try 14 (floating-point differences between computers change a fit), so its counts are left out.
- `scripts/bake_phase_results.ts` runs the in-browser model (Pyodide, as `bake_optimised.ts` does) on the best try after stages 1 and 2 and writes `src/searchReplay/phaseResults.json`. It stops unless each result's score, by the app's rule in `src/metrics.ts`, matches the trace's score to 1e-6 (all six matched). About 6.5 and 2 minutes per building; run one process per building in parallel, then join the parts: `node scripts/bake_phase_results.ts building_01` (and 02, 03), then `node scripts/bake_phase_results.ts --merge`. The default settings' simulation is the challenge's baseline (`challenge.json`), the final one our optimised result (`optimised.json`).

**Code:** `src/searchReplay/`: `replayTimeline.ts` (which tries and stage are on screen at a moment, best so far), `phaseMoments.ts` (the four moments and which one is shown), `usePlayClock.ts` (play when scrolled into view, pause, seek), `SearchReplay.tsx` (the player and the score chart), `BillsChart.tsx`, `searchReplay.css`. The settings' plain labels are shared with the city's settings panel in `src/panels/settingGroups.ts`; the pill switch is the shared `.site-switch` in `src/site/site.css`.

**Checks:** `replayTimeline.test.ts`: every logged try with its score, one final candidate (the best try), the three stages covering every try in order, Focus holding 6 settings at the best spread-out try's values, a timeline that starts empty, only moves forward and ends on every try. `phaseMoments.test.ts`: each moment is the best try at that point, its bars give its score by the challenge's rule, scores never fall, and the chart shows the defaults during stage 1, then each finished stage's best. Checked in headless Chrome at 1360 px and 390 px: plays to try 48, no console errors, no sideways scrolling ([run 029](../../../factory/runs/029-algorithm-phases-video.md)).

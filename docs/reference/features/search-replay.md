# Search replay

[Knowledge base](../../index.md) · [Features](index.md)

**Type:** Reference · Answers: what does the landing page's “How the search found the settings” show, where do its numbers come from, and how are they checked?

The landing page replays our best calibration run, `all_12__ard_bayesian_optimisation__budget48__2018+2019__b01+b02+b03__w12__afef0.5` (overall 97.55), epoch by epoch, with real data only. An epoch is one of the method's batches: it fits its guess on all tries so far, picks a batch where the expected improvement is highest, and runs it. A switch picks the building. The replay starts by itself once scrolled into view, once, and shows the finished search, still, when the device asks for reduced motion; play, pause and a position slider sit under it.

- **Phases and epochs** (top): one track, the three phase names over the epochs, every block as wide as its share of the tries; the current epoch fills up as its tries come in, and clicking an epoch jumps there. Under it, one sentence on the current phase (and at the end, the best try).
- **Up close: one setting** (left), drawn like the textbook picture of a Bayesian search. The same method runs on one setting, with the other 11 settings at our best try's values, in as many epochs as the real run. The black line is the real model's score at 17–18 points along the setting; every try (black dot) is one of those points. The violet line is the method's guess fitted through the tries so far (its own `_fit_gp`, on log(1 + loss)) and the band how unsure it is (± 2 spreads), pinched at each try. Each epoch plays as one movement: the guess morphs into its new fit, the next tries appear as rings on the guess (where a try is expected to gain the most, by expected improvement), then, while the epoch's tries run on the right, each drops from where the guess expected it onto the real score. Epoch 1 drops three spread-out tries (the default value, then two spread over the range) without a guess; each later epoch picks 2 tries the way the method picks its batches (highest expected improvement, then a pretend result there, its `_kriging_believer`, and again). The setting is, of those that change smoothly, the one the method's guess on all 48 tries rates most important: night temperature (the temperature kept outside the warm hours) for building 01, the fully heated share for 02 and 03 (their weekend heating hours rank higher, but the model uses them only in whole hours). A one-line note says what the setting is. In all three buildings it ends on the true top, which is our best try's own score.
- **The real run** (right): the 48 tries against their number, the best score so far as a rising step line, the best try as a ringed dark-violet dot, the phases shaded (the current one in pale violet) and the epochs as dashed borders.

**The method's stages** (`ard_bayesian_optimisation`): 1. spread out: try 1 is the model's default settings, tries 2–13 a Latin hypercube over the 12 settings; 2. focus: the 6 settings with the shortest fitted length scales are searched, the other 6 stay at the best try's values (one epoch); 3. fine-tune: all 12 settings, epoch by epoch. The method proposes 12 tries per epoch; the organizer's objective does not run or log a try whose heating schedule rounds to the same whole hours as an earlier one, so a logged epoch can be shorter (building 01: 13, 6 of 12, 10, 12, 7).

**Where the numbers come from:**

- `data/evaluation_trace.csv`: the run's evaluation trace (byte-identical to the backend's, given by the owner).
- `scripts/bake_search_replay.py` (`npm run replay`) writes `src/searchReplay/searchReplay.json`: each try's score and whether it is the final candidate; the stages, from the trace; the epochs, from replaying the method with the backend's own code (imported read-only from the run's `calibration_snapshot.py`, scikit-learn 1.9.1) and the run's seed (2026 + building number − 1) against the trace. Buildings 01 and 03 replay exactly; building 02 does not from try 14 (floating-point differences between computers change a fit), so its Fine-tune is cut into 3 equal epochs and the page says they are estimated. It also picks the one setting and the points where the real model is run.
- `scripts/bake_slice_truth.ts` runs the in-browser model along that setting (plus the best try's own value, whose score must match the trace's) and writes `src/searchReplay/sliceTruth.json`; one process per building and part, then `--merge` (54 runs, about 15 minutes in 9 processes).
- `scripts/bake_slice_search.py` (`npm run replay:slice`) runs the one-setting search on those points and writes `src/searchReplay/sliceSearch.json`.

**Code:** `src/searchReplay/`: `replayTimeline.ts` (the epoch timeline: what is on screen at a moment, best so far, where an epoch starts), `sliceSteps.ts` (what the one-setting chart shows at that moment), `usePlayClock.ts` (play when scrolled into view, pause, seek), `PhaseStrip.tsx`, `GuessChart.tsx`, `SearchReplay.tsx` (the player and the chart of tries), `searchReplay.css`. The settings' one-sentence explanations (`settingHint`) are shared with the city's settings panel too. The settings' plain labels are shared with the city's settings panel in `src/panels/settingGroups.ts`; the pill switch is the shared `.site-switch` in `src/site/site.css`.

**Checks:** `replayTimeline.test.ts`: every logged try with its score, one final candidate (the best try); the stages and epochs cover every try in order; Focus holds 6 settings at the best spread-out try's values; the timeline starts empty, plays epoch by epoch, only moves forward and ends on every try; jumping to an epoch lands on its start. `sliceSteps.test.ts`: the true line holds every planned point and our best try's logged score; every try is a true point and the guess passes near it; one step per epoch, each adding the picks of the step before; the best try never gets worse and ends at the true top; no guess in epoch 1, tries only ever added, the final guess fully in at the end. Checked in headless Chrome at 1360 px and 390 px: plays to try 48, no console errors, no sideways scrolling ([run 029](../../../factory/runs/029-algorithm-phases-video.md)).

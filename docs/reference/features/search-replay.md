# Search replay

[Knowledge base](../../index.md) · [Features](index.md)

**Type:** Reference · Answers: what does the landing page's “How the search found the settings” show, where do its numbers come from, and how are they checked?

The landing page replays our best calibration run, `all_12__ard_bayesian_optimisation__budget48__2018+2019__b01+b02+b03__w12__afef0.5` (overall 97.55), epoch by epoch, with real data only. An epoch is one of the method's batches: it fits its guess on all tries so far, picks a batch where the expected improvement is highest, and runs it. A switch picks the building; the best score so far is shown next to it. The replay starts by itself once scrolled into view, once, and shows the finished search, still, when the device asks for reduced motion; play, pause and a position slider sit under it.

- **Phases and epochs** (top): one track, the three phase names over the epochs, every block as wide as its share of the tries; the current epoch fills up as its tries come in, and clicking an epoch jumps there. Under it, one sentence on the current phase (and at the end, the best try).
- **The real run**: the 48 tries against their number, the score on the y-axis, the best score so far as a rising step line, the best try as a ringed dark-violet dot, the phases shaded (the current one in pale violet) and the epochs as dashed borders.

**The method's phases** (`ard_bayesian_optimisation`): 1. spread out: try 1 is the model's default settings, tries 2–13 a Latin hypercube over the 12 settings; 2. focus: the 6 settings with the shortest fitted length scales are searched, the other 6 stay at the best try's values (one epoch); 3. fine-tune: all 12 settings, epoch by epoch. The method proposes 12 tries per epoch; the organizer's objective does not run or log a try whose heating schedule rounds to the same whole hours as an earlier one, so a logged epoch can be shorter (building 01: 13, 6 of 12, 10, 12, 7).

**Where the numbers come from:** `data/evaluation_trace.csv` is the run's evaluation trace (byte-identical to the backend's, given by the owner). `scripts/bake_search_replay.py` (`npm run replay`) writes `src/searchReplay/searchReplay.json`: each try's score and whether it is the final candidate; the phases, from the trace (13 spread-out tries by the method's rule; Focus = the following tries that keep 6 settings at the best spread-out try's values); the epochs, from replaying the method with the backend's own code (imported read-only from the run's `calibration_snapshot.py`, scikit-learn 1.9.1) and the run's seed (2026 + building number − 1) against the trace. Buildings 01 and 03 replay exactly; building 02 does not from try 14 (floating-point differences between computers change a fit), so its Fine-tune is cut into 3 equal epochs. No simulation is run and nothing in `vendor/` is changed.

**Code:** `src/searchReplay/`: `replayTimeline.ts` (the epoch timeline: what is on screen at a moment, best so far, where an epoch starts), `usePlayClock.ts` (play when scrolled into view, pause, seek), `PhaseStrip.tsx`, `SearchReplay.tsx` (the player and the chart), `searchReplay.css`. The pill switch is the shared `.site-switch` in `src/site/site.css`.

**Checks:** `replayTimeline.test.ts`: every logged try with its score, one final candidate (the best try); the phases and epochs cover every try in order; Focus holds 6 settings at the best spread-out try's values; the timeline starts empty, plays epoch by epoch, only moves forward and ends on every try; jumping to an epoch lands on its start. Checked in headless Chrome at 1360 px and 390 px: plays to try 48, no console errors, no sideways scrolling ([run 029](../../../factory/runs/029-algorithm-phases-video.md)).

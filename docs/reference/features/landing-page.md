# Landing page and methods page

[Knowledge base](../../index.md) · [Features](index.md)

**Type:** Reference · Answers: what do the landing page and the methods page show, where do their pictures come from, and which brand rules do they follow?

The site opens on the landing page (`index.html`, code in `src/landing/`). The search methods are compared on their own page, `methods.html` (`src/methods/`). The 3D city is at `city.html`, and the original table of every run stays at `approaches.html`. Neither new page loads 3D code, so they open fast. These two pages and the All runs page share one frame, laid out like the INTEGRATES site, in `src/site/`: the header (the logo back to the landing page, “Methods”, “All runs”, “Open the city”, the current page marked), `PageBand.tsx` (the full-width violet band with the page's title and text, and tiles or a card on the right), `Tiles.tsx` (rounded tiles with one sharp corner: the band's column and the three small tiles hanging from a picture's or panel's square lower-left corner), the pale violet overlap band, grey bands and panels with violet headings (`site.css`), the dark closing band with the white mark, and the footer; the pages' addresses are in `src/site/pages.ts`. All three header links remain visible on phones. The All runs page uses the frame too: the band holds “Every approach we explored.” with the best-score card, then the parameter groups in a grey panel and the table on a grey band.

**Landing page**, laid out like the [INTEGRATES site](https://www.integratesproject.eu/) with Calibra's own logo and words. Above the fold, a full-width brand-violet band with text only: the sentence “Three buildings, their real heating bills, and a simulation tuned to meet them.”, a short explanation, the white main button “Open the city” and “Compare the methods”, and rounded decorative tiles on the right (each with one sharp corner; violets, white and one orange; hidden below 900 px). Below it, the still picture of the 3D city as a wide strip (16:7, cut evenly at the top and bottom) overlapping from the violet band into a pale violet one, with “01 Office”, “02 School” and “03 Office complex” next to their pins (hidden on phones) and three small tiles hanging from its square lower-left corner. Then “The three buildings” (violet headings) as white cards in a light grey panel, each with the app's own building portrait, construction year, floors and floor area, and the best run's score for that building with its distance from the bills; and “Look closer at the results” on a grey band, linking to the methods page and the All runs table. The main button is white rather than the INTEGRATES site's orange, because orange keeps its one meaning in Calibra's identity. No eyebrow labels.

**Pictures:** taken from the running app, not drawn. `scripts/capture_landing_images.mjs` opens `city.html` on a running dev server in headless Chrome (software WebGL), waits for the sidebar's portraits, saves them as `public/landing/building_0N.png`, projects each 3D pin through the scene's camera (the dev build exposes it as `window.__three`) into `src/landing/cityPins.json`, then hides everything but the map and saves `public/landing/city.jpg`. Run it again with `node scripts/capture_landing_images.mjs` after the city or the buildings change.

**Methods page** (“Which search method gets closest to the bills?”, `MethodChart.tsx`): the title and text in the violet band, the chart card overlapping into the pale violet band, then the closing band. In the chart, one row per search method, ordered by its best run. Every run is a dot on the score line; the best run of each method is the large, solid optimised-violet dot with its score on the right, and the other runs are smaller light-violet dots. A switch shows the overall score or one building. The line starts at a multiple of 5 just below the weakest method's best run (`chartFloor` in `methodRanking.ts`); runs below it sit as open circles at the left edge, and the note under the chart says how many. Hovering or focusing a dot shows its score, its distance from the bills, its parameter group and variant. The runs come from `src/approaches/results.json`, the same data as the approaches page.

**Brand:** both follow the [corporate identity](../../../public/brand/calibra-corporate-identity.pdf) ([decision 0005](../../decisions/0005-calibra-brand.md)): no eyebrow labels; Sora 600 for headings and big numbers with tabular figures, Inter for text; white page, light grey cards, thin lines, no heavy shadows; one black main button; Lucide icons at 16 px. Each colour keeps its meaning, so the chart uses only optimised violet for runs and orange appears only in the flame; parameter groups are not colour-coded.

**Search replay:** after “The three buildings”, “How the search found the settings” replays the best run try by try; see [Search replay](search-replay.md). It shows the best score over the 48 tries for that run, not for every run (the results CSV has no per-run history).

**Checks:** `src/methods/methodRanking.test.ts` (ranking by method and by building, the chart floor). Headless Chrome at 1360 px and at a 390 px phone width: no sideways scrolling, no console errors.
